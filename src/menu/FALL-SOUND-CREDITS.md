# Falling creature voice

Recorded groan: `ooh.ogg` from **80 CC0 creature SFX** by rubberduck.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx
License: CC0 / public domain.
Project asset: `assets/fall-groan.ogg` (original compressed recording).

Playback is gently pitched up, normalized and faded; a synthesized padded ground thump and dust puff follow at 0.95 seconds. The result panel opens at 2 seconds. The voice preloads with the nature audio; if unavailable, a short synthesized vocal fallback remains. No new remote runtime dependency is required.
